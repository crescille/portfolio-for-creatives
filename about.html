About me

I'm Crescille Angel, a branding designer. I love looking at an idea from different perspectives until I land on a visual direction that fits what the piece needs to say. I play and experiment along the way but stay grounded and consistent, so the concept never gets lost. My heart's in work that builds connection and community.

When I'm not designing, I enjoy getting outdoors, listening to music, and taking a dance class or two. I'm always looking for new ways to express myself creatively, and every new step I learn finds its way back into my work.
