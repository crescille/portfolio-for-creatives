About me

I'm Crescille Angel Buyuccan, a branding designer. I love looking at an idea from different perspectives until I land on a visual direction that fits what the piece needs to say. I play and experiment along the way but stay grounded and consistent, so the concept never gets lost. My heart's in works that builds connection and community.

When I’m not designing, you can usually find me touching grass, spending time in nature, listening to music, or taking a dance class or two. I’m always finding new ways to express myself creatively, and often, what I explore outside of design finds its way back into my work.
