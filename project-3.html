Kasama Filipino Restaurant

Branding / Print Media / Web Design

Kasama is a fictional Filipino family restaurant founded by the Humiwat family in Vancouver in 1987. For some, it's a taste of home. For others, it's a first introduction to Filipino food and culture. Rooted in the warmth of shared meals and family traditions, this project brings that feeling to life through a complete brand identity, scrapbook-style menu, collage-inspired jar set, mailer box, and website.
