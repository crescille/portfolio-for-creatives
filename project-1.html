Cerv

Branding / Packaging / Web Design

Cerv reimagines beer as a form of self-expression. The concept centers on a fun, interactive package design that lets drinkers "choose their mood" by selecting a beer through a specific hand gesture paired with a corresponding colour scheme.

It's a small ritual that builds a deeper emotional connection between the brand and its drinkers, turning every sip into a memorable experience.
